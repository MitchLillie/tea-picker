# Tea Picker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Tea Picker is a Shop Mini that runs inside the Shop app. It recommends traditional Chinese, Taiwanese and Japanese teas based on your answers to a short tasting survey or a tea you name.

## What we collect

**Nothing that identifies you.** Tea Picker has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. We never see your name, email, address, payment details, location or device identifiers.

## What is stored, and where

So the Mini remembers you between visits, it saves the following **on your device, in the Shop app's storage for this Mini**:

- your tasting-survey answers (for example, preferred oxidation, aromas, body);
- your settings (music on or off, volume, motion, how tea names are shown);
- whether you have already seen the opening animation.

This information stays on your device. We cannot access it, and it is not sold, shared or used for advertising or profiling.

## Product search

When Tea Picker shows tea products, it asks the Shop app to search Shop's catalogue for tea names (for example, "Dragon Well green tea"). These searches are handled by Shop under **Shop's own privacy policy**; Tea Picker does not receive or store any information about you from them. Viewing, saving or buying a product happens in Shop and is governed by Shop's policies and those of the merchant.

## Deleting your data

- In Tea Picker, open **Rest › Forget my palate** to delete your survey answers.
- Removing the Mini from the Shop app, or clearing the Shop app's data, deletes everything Tea Picker stored.
- You can also email us at mlillie87@gmail.com and we will help, though we hold no data about you on our side.

## Children

Tea Picker is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
